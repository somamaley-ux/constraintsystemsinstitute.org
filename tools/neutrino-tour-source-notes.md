# Neutrino tour: source and model notes

The tour presents the supplied neutrino carrier arc. All page locators below are physical PDF pages, including front matter, in the local versions read for the tour. The user supplied the DOI targets. A DOI record becoming temporarily unavailable does not change its destination.

## Scope carried into the public explanation

The final carrier and prediction theorem concerns two completed source-qualified vectorlike branches: the D3-1.3 successor target, D4 public-summary target, and either bounded accidental B−L through operator dimension four or exact global B−L. Other parent branches remain open. Within each accepted realization there is one complete carrier; across realizations there remain at least continuum many standing classes. One complete carrier is not a unique selected microscopic realization.

The exact central construction contains three distinct light standing sectors and one rank-three heavy standing sector. Splitting heavy structural support does not itself produce another complete carrier. Carrier persistence under perturbation requires its own certified trace-transport cell; the tour's matrix illustration does not compute or certify such a cell.

Authoritative final wording: [PCR8](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22747926), PDF pp. 21–26, theorems P8-12 through P8-16. The final text calls the heavy object **structural support**, and explicitly declines to promote it to an unstable physical pole. This terminology takes precedence over older shorthand in PCR6/PCR7.

## 1. Compensated threshold

Source: [PCR5B](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22735684), PDF pp. 61–64, equations 83–85 and Lemmas 16.1–16.3.

The source family is `Mλ = λ M0`, `Yλ = sqrt(λ) Y0`, so `Yλ Mλ^-1 Yλ^T = κ*`. The matching threshold changes while the fixed target-facing low-energy coefficient stays unchanged. The source proves an injection from the open interval `(1,2)` into the standing classes. This is a continuum of alternative realizations of one target-relative role, not a continuum of neutrino species.

`threshold(lambda)` is the scalar, dimensionless slice `M0 = Y0 = 1`. Its slider includes the two endpoints only for ease of use; the published continuum witness uses the open interval. The implementation makes no measured mass assignment or claim that the neutrino theory is a one-dimensional model.

## 2. Light–heavy accounting

Sources: [PCR7](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22747744), PDF pp. 15–28; [PCR8](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22747926), PDF pp. 21–22, P8-13.

At the same accepted source point `N = c_E U`, `V_H = −s_E U`, `c_E² + s_E² = 1`, and `U U† = I3`. Therefore `Δactive = I3 − N N† = Ωheavy = V_H V_H† = δ_E I3`, with `δ_E = s_E² > 0`, rank three and zero closure residual. A complete test also needs structural support, the off-diagonal/correlation information, shared normalization, and all three flavors; three bars alone do not establish the whole packet.

`strength(delta, observedHeavy)` displays the diagonal Gram weights in a simple chart. The optional heavy-weight array is a comparison record: mismatches remain visible and are not silently repaired. The initial `delta = 0.003` is chosen for legibility and is **illustrative, not measured and not the source certificate's specified value**. The source-qualified central bound `0 < δ_E <= 1/256` appears in PCR8 PDF pp. 27–29 and PCR7.5 PDF pp. 47–50. It is not a universal experimental bound or a numerical lower bound. Zero is accepted by this teaching API only to depict the zero reference; it leaves the marked positive child.

## 3. Independent perturbations

Sources: [PCR7.5](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22747820), PDF pp. 11–39; [PCR8](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22747926), PDF pp. 22–25, P8-14/P8-15.

The tour varies two independent coordinates:

- Relative structural source-mass support is `(1−ε, 1, 1+ε)`, `ε = 0.18 × split`. These are dimensionless source-spectrum coordinates, not physical propagator poles, reconstructed detector peaks, GeV values or lifetime predictions. This is one simple three-way splitting path; the paper also allows two-plus-one splitting.
- With `a = 0.6 × anisotropy`, the heavy coupling block is `V′ = −diag(sqrt(δ(1+a)), sqrt(δ), sqrt(δ(1−a)))`. Define `N′ = diag(sqrt(1−δ(1+a)), sqrt(1−δ), sqrt(1−δ(1−a)))`. Then `N′N′† + V′V′† = I3` exactly in this explicitly constructed family. `Δ′ = Ω′` persists while anisotropy breaks flavor universality. The family is positive and full-rank throughout its controls, with `||V′−V|| < sqrt(δ)`.

The uniform source bound on the central `δ` does not assert that each perturbed row stays below `1/256`; the perturbation view is an illustrative isometric family, not a full source-survival or empirical-acceptance calculation. All source/domain and carrier-cell obligations remain separate. The API returns `carrierStatus: 'conditional'` rather than inferring terminal cardinality from its matrices. No change of this control is presented as an actual perturbation of nature.

Exact degeneracy and exact universality have zero unrestricted symmetry-breaking radius. The tutorial must not imply that mass degeneracy causes universality, or that either one proves closure or one-carrier identity. These have independent hypotheses in the paper.

## 4. What makes a test informative

Sources: [PCR7.5](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22747820), PDF pp. 31–41, 55–71; [PCR8](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22747926), PDF pp. 25–31, P8-16 and experimental outcome discussion.

`experiment(options)` is a deterministic **toy common-record comparison**, not an event generator, fitted dataset, facility sensitivity calculation, or instantiated SPIC. It declares one scalar common-normalization response, uses one displayed error radius, and compares the toy records `[δ]` and `[0]`. In this declared scalar response only, the record separation is `δ`; the chart centers and its decision rule use this same separation. The real structural pair-space gap and a real detector's record-space separation cannot be equated without a response certificate.

The chosen radius is `systematic + noise/sqrt(exposure)` in dimensionless record units. Exposure is relative and does not represent real events or luminosity. A capable toy separation requires the strict inequality `2 × radius < separation`. Increasing exposure reduces only the statistical term; if the systematic floor reaches half the separator, no exposure fixes it. Missing a flavor or the heavy support produces `incomplete`, even at low uncertainty. These coverage gates teach a requirement of the real protocol; this one-dimensional illustration does not implement the complete flavor, correlation, and support record. `capable` says only that these two declared toy records can be distinguished at the displayed radius; it is neither a discovery nor a carrier-packet match.

The real framework additionally needs same-record response and inverse conditioning, all relevant correlations, nuisance/control/sideband inference, hypothesis-independent availability, sampling/coverage, support-resolution guards and independent held-out data. Current-facility SPIC remains NotReady in PCR8. A positive match does not uniquely select the microscopic source; inadequate sensitivity neither confirms nor falsifies it.

## Numerical authority

The separate D3 **28–30 GeV / 0.2 mm** search benchmark is not the final VJ carrier's derived mass/lifetime. The final VJ heavy domain `H(I_E)` remains symbolic; the physical pole/lifetime and D3-to-VJ bridges have their own missing inputs. The tour models use no GeV or millimeter defaults and make no current-experiment discovery promise. See PCR8 PDF pp. 27–29 and PCR7.5 PDF pp. 42–60.
