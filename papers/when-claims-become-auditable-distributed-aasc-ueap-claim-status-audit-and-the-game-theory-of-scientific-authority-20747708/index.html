When Claims Become Auditable: Distributed AASC/UEAP Claim-Status Audit and the Game Theory of Scientific Authority

Amos Jay Maley · 119 pages · Scientific claim audit

Read paperDownload PDFDOI

This reference edition proposes distributed AASC/UEAP audit of institutional rigor claims and a conditional game-theoretic exposure model. Portable execution, consequential detection and the stated independence assumptions determine what becomes auditable. Execution alone does not earn authority, and a mismatch is not a finding of falsity or misconduct. Multi-evaluator calibration is not presented as complete.
