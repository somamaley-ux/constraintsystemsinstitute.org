Dimensionless Gauge Couplings as Moduli

Amos Jay Maley · 4 pages · Gauge couplings and parameter status

Read paperDownload PDFDOI

Within renormalizable four-dimensional quantum field theory and absent extra ultraviolet, dynamical, boundary or empirical inputs, the manuscript treats dimensionless gauge couplings as continuous structurally admissible parameters. It argues that Gaussian fixed points do not uniquely fix them and states stronger isolated-fixed-point conditions. Moduli is used structurally, without a smooth-manifold claim. The conclusion is confined to the stated framework.
