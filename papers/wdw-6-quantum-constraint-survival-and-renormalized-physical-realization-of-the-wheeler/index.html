Complete publisher description
Description Quantum Constraint Survival and Renormalized Physical Realization of the Wheeler–DeWitt Family is the sixth paper in the Wheeler–DeWitt ARC. It takes the regulator-indexed, constraint-native candidate families constructed in WDW‑5 and tests which, if any, survive as lawful quantum realizations. Its central question is: Given separately typed Wheeler–DeWitt candidate families with defined configuration carriers, regulated constraint actions, and algebraic representation seeds, what further conditions are required for a candidate to survive quantum closure and attain renormalized physical realization? The manuscript carries the candidate families through the quantum obligations that WDW‑5 explicitly leaves open: regulator control, constraint-commutator closure, anomaly analysis, compatible representation and domain structure, and the construction of a physically meaningful constrained realization. Survival is not assigned merely because a regulated operator can be written or because a formal algebraic-dual solution exists. Its central passage is: constraint-native regulated candidate families⟶quantum constraint survival⟶renormalized physical Wheeler–DeWitt realization. The paper preserves the inherited WDW architecture: connection-typed canonical provenance, proper-redescription descent, constrained refoliation, joint classical gravity–matter occupancy, and the differentiated configuration-space structure. A proposed quantum realization must retain those constraint roles through renormalization rather than replacing them with a presentation-dependent operator prescription or an unqualified choice of representative. The endpoint is a surviving renormalized Wheeler–DeWitt family with physically admissible constrained realization data. It does not treat the result as an ordinary externally timed Schrödinger theory, nor does it make a clock choice, foliation, or semiclassical reduction primitive. Those relational, covariant–canonical, joint-kernel, and clock-neutral closure obligations belong to the subsequent WDW papers. WDW‑6 is thus the ARC’s quantum-survival threshold: it converts WDW‑5’s technically specified candidate level into the renormalized physical input required for the later profile-clock, semiclassical-recovery, and final global-closure stages.
