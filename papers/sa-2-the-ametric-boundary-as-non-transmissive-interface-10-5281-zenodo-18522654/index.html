SA_2 The AMetric Boundary as Non Transmissive Interface

Amos Jay Maley

This paper establishes the AMetric Boundary as a non-transmissive interface separating admissible description from illicit scope transport. The boundary is shown to be structural rather than dynamical: nothing crosses it by inference, extrapolation, parameter transport, or representational continuity. By exhaustion, any attempt to treat the boundary as permeable-whether via limiting procedures, emergent narratives, or meta-descriptive projection-violates admissibility and fails closed.
