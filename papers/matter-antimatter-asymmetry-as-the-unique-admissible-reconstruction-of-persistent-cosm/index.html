Matter–Antimatter Asymmetry as the Unique Admissible Reconstruction of Persistent Cosmological Identity

Amos Jay Maley

Overview This paper develops an AASC account of matter-antimatter asymmetry as a structural reconstruction theorem rather than a new baryogenesis mechanism. It does not alter Standard Model dynamics, derive the baryon-to-photon ratio, compute CP-sector numerical values, or select a specific physical mechanism such as leptogenesis or electroweak baryogenesis.
