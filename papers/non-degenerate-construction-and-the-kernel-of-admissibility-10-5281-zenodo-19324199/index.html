From actual determinate objects and incidences, the paper argues for the necessary functions of Reference, Standing, Admissibility and Irreversibility. It characterizes exact representations by preservation of every required original observation.

What this edition establishes

Two stones can touch without an observer or a surviving record. The constitutive argument asks what would be lost if the relata, qualification, joint conditions or original occurrence could be replaced. One mechanism may perform several Kernel functions.

For an independently specified task, an exact reader exists precisely when presentations with the same code agree on every required original observation. At the same independently identified bearer and complete question, two individually warranted answers agree when the original result relation is single-valued.

The appendices develop finite authorization, quotients, descent, support and symmetry, and identify formal counterparts in Lean. These applications retain the original incidence and task conditions; a catalogue update is not a new independent Lean build.
