Module A: operational metric-source attachment

Amos Jay Maley

About this paper

Operational Metric–Source Attachment is Module A of the post–structural-unification continuation of the gravity–quantum unification ARC.

Structural Unification of Einstein and Schrödinger Dynamics establishes that the gravitational and quantum descriptions are lawful projections of one admissible physical interior. It also identifies a remaining obligation: the metric projection cannot be coupled to matter by merely inserting an independently postulated stress–energy tensor. The source must be constructed from admissible operational data and attached to the metric response through a standing-preserving bridge.

Module A discharges that obligation. It constructs the operational metric–source interface required to turn structural unification into a source-complete gravitational realization.

Scope and dependencies

This distinction is essential to the ARC. Structural unification establishes that Einstein and Schrödinger dynamics belong to one physical interior, but common origin alone does not authorize an arbitrary transfer of source content between their projections. A lawful bridge must identify what generates the source, what survives quotienting, how local presentations compose, and why the resulting tensor can act in the metric equation without circularity.

The manuscript provides that bridge. Matter does not appear merely as a tensor written on the opposite side of the Einstein equation; its source-bearing content is reconstructed from admissible operational structure and attached to the gravitational projection through the common interior.
