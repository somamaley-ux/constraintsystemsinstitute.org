The Structure of Admissibility

Bivalent Qualification, Exhaustion, and the Unique Admissible Interior

Amos Jay Maley · 46 pages

Constructs the canonical minimal observation interface and the unique greatest safe interior for an independently specified reuse contract. Qualification, information, coverage and warrant remain distinct requirements.

What this edition establishes

A bridge can pass one load test and fail the next. One initial qualification bit is exact only when every required outcome, including definedness, agrees within each bit value. Complete observation profiles determine the minimal interface; finite profile counts give the least supplementary-label bound.

Standing through every declared continuation is the greatest safe interior. Every excluded configuration has a finite path containing a failed qualification or unwarranted edge. The paper characterizes exact preservation of the one-step safety operator, including nondeterministic continuations.

Exhaustion compares proposed results with original incidences and independently specified uses. A reader can answer every received case correctly while omitting an entire required case; coverage is a separate condition from correctness on represented inputs.

Scope and limits
The interior may be empty. Uniqueness concerns the complete/maximal interior for fixed data, not every safe subset or a unique physical occupant. Failure retention and negative endpoint absorption have different conditions.
