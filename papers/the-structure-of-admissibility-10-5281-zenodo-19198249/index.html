The Structure of Admissibility

Amos Jay Maley

Primitive Paper 2 of 2 Overview This paper is the second paper in the primitive AASC series. The preceding kernel paper establishes the fixed-domain governance roles required for non-degenerate construction: admissibility, standing, reference, and irreversibility. The present paper assumes that kernel result and studies the next question: once the AASC interface is operative, what structure must admissibility itself have?
