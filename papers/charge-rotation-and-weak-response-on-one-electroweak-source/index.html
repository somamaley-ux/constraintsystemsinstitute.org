Charge, Rotation, and Weak Response on One Electroweak Source

Admissible redescription, dynamical calibration, and a finite quantum record

Amos Jay Maley · 31 pages

Read paperDownload PDFPhilPapers record

A single Gauss-constrained electroweak preparation supports electric charge, a marked orbital contrast and a finite weak-energy test. The paper follows the calibration under local evolution and constructs a finite quantum receiver.

Three readings of one source

The construction starts with explicit smooth data in the bosonic one-doublet electroweak theory. With phase-locked momentum, the readings share a spatial measure. Other preparations on the same constraint surface agree in total charge, rotation and weak readings but differ in their marked orbital contrast.

What changes when the source evolves

The path contrast follows the instantaneous charge density. The original weak controls need new compensating momenta when the source evolves. The paper exhibits second-order drift from the initial calibration and derives the marked current term that accounts for it.

A finite receiver

Selected symplectic modes of the constrained preparations give finite quantum operators and a binary receiver with controlled pulse and readout errors. This is a specified detector model, not a laboratory result or a quantization of the full continuum electroweak flow.
