Invariance Constraints and the Non-Predictivity of Zero-Point Vacuum Energy in Quantum Field Theory

Amos Jay Maley · 11 pages · Vacuum-energy invariance companion

Read paperDownload PDFDOI

The manuscript binds structural invariance language to the homogeneous metric-proportional vacuum sector of QFT and semiclassical gravitational matching. A regulator-dependent absolute matter-sector offset does not supply a standalone invariant cosmological prediction. Relational Casimir forces, phase differences and response quantities remain available; compensating shifts preserve the total matched cosmological coefficient. The analysis does not classify the full curved-spacetime stress tensor or solve naturalness, radiative stability or matching.
