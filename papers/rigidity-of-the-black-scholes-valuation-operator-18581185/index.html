Rigidity of the Black–Scholes Valuation Operator

Amos Jay Maley · 23 pages · Valuation operator rigidity

Read paperDownload PDFDOI

Within the fixed payoff-linear, deterministic one-factor local valuation class with its self-financing, scale-covariant and time-homogeneous conditions, the manuscript derives the Black-Scholes operator form. Positive volatility remains a calibration parameter. It does not claim that empirical markets are uniquely lognormal or exclude models with jumps, frictions, stochastic rates or stochastic volatility.
