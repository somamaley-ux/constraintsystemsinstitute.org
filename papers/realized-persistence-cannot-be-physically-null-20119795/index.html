Realized Persistence Cannot Be Physically Null

Amos Jay Maley · 29 pages · Realized Physics Arc - Paper 1

Read paperDownload PDFDOI

The manuscript argues that, within a fixed admissible regime, minimally realized standing-bearing persistence requires non-identical continuation-sensitive reconstruction classes. This non-null interior is a role-equivalence class, not a selected substance, geometry, field or particle.
