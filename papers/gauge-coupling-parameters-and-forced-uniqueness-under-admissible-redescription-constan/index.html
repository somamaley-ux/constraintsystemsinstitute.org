Gauge-Coupling Parameters and Forced Uniqueness under Admissible Redescription (Constants)

Amos Jay Maley

Overview This paper examines whether gauge-coupling parameters can carry forced uniqueness under admissible redescription. It argues that raw numeric gauge-coupling values are chart-level quantities whose values vary under admissible reparameterization, and therefore cannot function as standing-bearing invariants. Negative Result Admissibility constraints do not collapse continuous gauge-coupling freedom to a unique numeric value.
