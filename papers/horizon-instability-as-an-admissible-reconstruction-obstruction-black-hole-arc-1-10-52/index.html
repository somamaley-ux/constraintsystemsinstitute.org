Overview This manuscript is Paper I of the AASC Black-Hole Endpoint Arc . It proves the exterior no-totalization theorem for uncertified horizon reconstruction. The paper asks a narrow question: Can an uncertified permanent horizon remain a continuing same-scope standing-preserving exterior reconstruction object? The answer is no.
