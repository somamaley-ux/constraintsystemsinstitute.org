WDW 3: Normal Deformation as Constrained Refoliation

Amos Jay Maley

About this paper

Normal Deformation as Constrained Refoliation is the third paper in the Wheeler–DeWitt ARC. It establishes the normal-deformation and refoliation structure required for the representation-stable canonical gravity realization obtained through WDW‑1 and WDW‑2.

When an authenticated Einstein–source realization is presented on different admissible hypersurfaces, under what conditions does normal deformation preserve one constrained physical realization rather than generate a slicing-dependent replacement?

The manuscript treats normal deformation as a constrained refoliation problem. Building on the connection-typed canonical projection of WDW‑1 and the proper-redescription groupoid descent of WDW‑2, it develops the lapse-directed normal and shift-directed tangential deformation roles together with the constraint relations required for their lawful composition.

Scope and dependencies

WDW‑3 is therefore not a fixed-slice construction and does not make any preferred foliation physically fundamental. Its load-bearing contribution is to establish a controlled refoliation structure on the globally descended canonical realization, so that subsequent gravity–matter and quantum-constraint stages inherit lawful continuation rather than an uncontrolled collection of slice-labelled representatives.

The paper does not yet integrate classical matter occupancy, construct the configuration-space Wheeler–DeWitt candidate family, establish quantum constraint survival, or provide final physical realization. Those are obligations of the later WDW papers.
