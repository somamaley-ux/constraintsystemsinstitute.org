Self-Adjoint Extension Selection Under Quadratic Form Order In Quantum Mechanics

Amos Jay Maley · 40 pages · Self-adjoint extension selection

Read paperDownload PDFDOI

The manuscript gives a fixed-base classification of extension and readback procedures, then applies it to self-adjoint operators. Redescription invariance alone does not choose a unique nontrivial extension in the stated deficiency setting. For a lower-semibounded symmetric operator, its explicit closed-form dominance criterion characterizes the Friedrichs endpoint; the nonnegative Krein endpoint uses the dual criterion. Boundary data and framework changes remain distinct alternatives. No new empirical physics is asserted.
