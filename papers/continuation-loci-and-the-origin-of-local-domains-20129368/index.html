Continuation Loci and the Origin of Local Domains

Amos Jay Maley · 19 pages · Realized Physics Arc - Paper 3

Read paperDownload PDFDOI

The manuscript derives pre-topological continuation-neighborhoods and local domains from bounded standing-localization loci, fixed boundary traces and admitted standing-relevant continuation. Boundary compatibility alone does not imply adjacency. Admitted adjacency paths generate local continuation domains without supplying metric distance, open-set topology or spacetime causality.
