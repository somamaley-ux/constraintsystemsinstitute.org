Renormalization Invariance and Standing Preservation in the Non-Predictivity of Zero-Point Vacuum Energy

Amos Jay Maley · 8 pages · Vacuum-energy invariance companion

Read paperDownload PDFDOI

The manuscript supplies a local correspondence theorem: in the specified homogeneous metric-proportional vacuum sector, standing preservation and descent to the QFT renormalization quotient select the same scalar assignments. The equivalence is conditional on its defined binding and transformation class. It preserves empirical boundary input, Casimir and phase differences, and the unresolved effective cosmological-constant problem. It neither derives QFT from AASC nor extends this classification to every curved-spacetime stress-tensor contribution.
