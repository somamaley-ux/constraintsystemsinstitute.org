Primitive Closure, Skin Openness, BSM Audit, and No Same-Scope Primitive Escape This paper completes the Standard Model carrier-exhaustion sequence. It asks whether any same-scope Standard Model-associated attribute retains primitive standing outside the certified carrier package after the gauge, matter, charge, coupling, renormalization, flavor, mixing, generation, Higgs, and structural-spine closures have been received. The method is an AASC primitive-status exhaustion proof. It distinguishes primitive carrier standing from derivative skins, coordinate displays, routes, projections, numerical statuses, evidence records, BSM surfaces, and target updates under the kernel conditions required for non-degenerate physical claimhood. The paper receives: the master classifier; the gauge-sector carrier closure; the matter-sector carrier closure; the charge, coupling, and renormalization closure; the flavor, mixing, and generation closure; the open-generation cardinality endpoint; the Higgs bridge and fixation endpoint; the AASC kernel and admissibility source; the standalone Standard Model structural spine. The kernel is not treated as an interpretive overlay placed on physics. It is the minimal non-degeneracy condition under which physical claims retain standing, reference, sameness, comparison class, prediction, and falsification. The global primitive carrier package The certified primitive package is assembled as C_SM^prim = C_gauge^prim ∪ C_matter^prim ∪ C_CCR^prim ∪ C_flavor^prim ∪ {H_br/fix}. It contains the primitive gauge, matter, charge/coupling/renormalization, flavor, generation-governance, and Higgs-bridge structures already closed by the preceding sequence. The paper distinguishes this package from the much larger collection of physically meaningful Standard Model data. Numerical parameters, masses, widths, running couplings, mixing angles, CP phases, detector records, effective operators, projection coordinates, and measured anomalies may all be physically significant without thereby becoming additional primitive carriers. The registered status atlas preserves these derivative and downstream structures rather than deleting them. It classifies them as registered representatives, measurements, transport data, projection results, EFT coordinates, burdens, extensions, target updates, or failure dispositions according to their source and authority. Global coverage and no leakage A global coverage theorem assigns every same-scope Standard Model-associated attribute to an exhaustive status class: descent to the certified primitive carrier package; registered nonprimitive status; generation governance; a burden of proof; failed primitive promotion; a hostile certificate branch; or a target update. The no-leakage theorem prevents generation governance, numerical values, projections, EFT displays, anomaly records, or BSM surfaces from silently becoming primitive carrier identity. The decision procedure and normal-form theorem ensure that no hidden default primitive is introduced merely because an attribute is useful, irreducible within a representation, or associated with a familiar physical label. Final residual deletion theorem The central endpoint is the empty-survivor result: Survₚᵣᵢₘᴰˢᴹ³(Attr(D_SM³) \ C_SM^prim) = ∅. Equivalently, the residual same-scope primitive survivor count is zero. The proof proceeds by contradiction. Any proposed residual primitive attribute must belong to one of the registered constructor classes. The global coverage theorem then assigns it to the certified carrier package, registered status, generation governance, burden, failed primitive status, or hostile branch. The hostile branch is explicitly exposed rather than absorbed. A valid same-scope primitive outside the certified package would defeat SM-6 by breaking an imported closure input, constructor adequacy, role-necessity exhaustion, target classification, or an SM-6 bridge lemma. Under the fixed dependency package and constructor-presented arena, no such hostile certificate closes. Primitive closure with skin openness The resulting distinction is essential: primitive carrier architecture is closed at the same-scope level; empirical measurements remain open; numerical fits remain open; effective-field-theory descriptions remain open; anomaly reports remain open; BSM surfaces remain open; new projection languages remain open; target-updating physics remains open. The theorem is therefore not empirical finality, not a no-new-physics thesis, and not a derivation of every numerical Standard Model parameter. It establishes primitive closure while preserving openness at the level of derivative descriptions, measurements, realizations, and genuinely changed targets. Falsifiability and hostile reconstruction The closure theorem is falsifiable within its proof class. A valid counterexample would need to preserve the same target, comparison class, reference standing, and kernel conditions while supplying a genuinely primitive same-scope role outside the certified package. A new measurement, anomaly, EFT coefficient, BSM model, or changed regime does not defeat the theorem merely by being physically real or theoretically useful. It becomes relevant to SM-6 only if it survives the same-scope primitive-status classification or explicitly updates the target. The paper therefore retains a hostile reconstruction protocol, evidence-tier register, dependency ledger, carrier register, BSM/EFT audit matrix, target-update ledger, and Lean 4 formalization target. Scope and realization boundary The theorem closes same-scope primitive residue, skin-to-primitive promotion, unlicensed parameter and projection primitiveness, unlicensed BSM insertion, unlicensed Higgs escape, unlicensed flavor or generation escape, and unlicensed cross-sector interface primitiveness. It does not close empirical refinement, new measurements, precision fits, EFT coefficients, anomaly reports, BSM target updates, new physical regimes, numerical derivations, or physics outside the declared Standard Model carrier arena. Role in the Standard Model carrier-exhaustion sequence SM-6 is the capstone of the Standard Model carrier-exhaustion arc. It integrates the preceding sector closures and applies the global classifier to prove that no same-scope primitive carrier survives outside the certified package. Its final interpretation is primitive closure with skin openness: the Standard Model carrier domain is exhausted at the same-scope primitive level, while measurement, projection, anomaly, EFT, BSM, and target-updating physics remain available for further investigation.
