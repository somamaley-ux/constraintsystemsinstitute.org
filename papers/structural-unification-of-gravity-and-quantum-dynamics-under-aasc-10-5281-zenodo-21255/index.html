Overview Structural Unification of Einstein and Schrödinger Dynamics is the first capstone of the gravity–quantum unification ARC. It integrates the independently derived Einstein and Schrödinger normal forms by proving that they are not laws of two primitively separate physical interiors. They are distinct, lawful projections of one prior admissibility-bearing physical interior. The manuscript asks: Given the independently established gravitational and quantum projections, what common structure must exist for both to belong to one determinate physical reality without illicit transfer, duplication, or loss of standing? Its answer is structural rather than a new mixed field equation. Singleton admissible-interior closure excludes separate gravitational and quantum interiors, while the projection and joint-ledger constructions establish the conditions under which Einstein and Schrödinger dynamics can coexist as faithful developments of one common interior. The unification result does not collapse one equation into the other. It proves instead that: the gravitational and quantum theorem objects arise from one realized physical interior; each projection preserves its own carrier, standing conditions, and lawful continuation; neither projection may acquire primitive authority over the common interior; cross-projection interaction must be mediated through a lawful joint ledger; any proposed coupling that violates the common dependency structure fails within the declared same-scope regime. The result is therefore a structural unification theorem: one interior, two faithful dynamical projections, and one shared burden of compatibility. Coupling-Exhaustion Result A central contribution is the classification of proposed gravity–quantum couplings that do not preserve the established dependency order. Within the manuscript’s declared scope, a noncompliant construction must fall into one or more of the following categories: Backflow: a downstream projection is used to manufacture or alter its own upstream standing conditions. Laundering: information or authority is transferred between projections without a lawful standing-bearing bridge. Declared scope change: the construction introduces genuinely new structure and therefore belongs to a richer target rather than the original fixed comparison class. Skin: the apparent coupling changes only representation, coordinates, labels, or other non-standing-bearing presentation data. Standing failure: the proposed construction does not preserve the identity, admissibility, reference, or continuation required of the target. This exhaustion result does not prohibit all interaction. It specifies what a lawful interaction must preserve and separates genuine richer-scope continuation from illegitimate same-scope coupling. Relation to the Einstein and Schrödinger Papers The two preceding dynamical papers derive parallel but distinct first-response normal forms: Einstein dynamics governs the minimal lawful metric response of the gravitational projection. Schrödinger dynamics governs the minimal lawful Hamiltonian continuation of the closed quantum projection. Neither projection independently constitutes the whole admissible physical interior. Neither may generate the primitive standing of the other. Their unity lies upstream, in the common interior from which both descend. Their interaction requires a lawful shared structure rather than an informal identification of variables or sources. The present manuscript converts this parallelism into a genuine unification architecture. It explains why the equations belong to the same physical reality without falsely treating them as identical descriptions or forcing one to be derived directly from the other. Reframing Quantum Gravity The manuscript changes the primitive formulation of the quantum-gravity problem. If Einstein and Schrödinger dynamics are downstream projections of one prior physical interior, then quantizing the Einstein projection alone does not reconstruct the common origin of gravity and quantum standing. It acts on one already-derived representation. The appropriate unification task is instead: construct a common standing-bearing upstream object; preserve the independently established gravitational and quantum projections; define lawful source, exchange, record, and compatibility relations; prevent backflow and cross-projection laundering; certify the resulting joint realization relative to a fixed target. On this account, a quantum-gravity formalism may still arise downstream, but it must be shown to be a faithful representation or continuation of the common architecture rather than presumed to be the primitive source of that architecture. Scope and Nonclaims The manuscript does not claim: an independent rederivation of the universal AASC kernel; that the Einstein and Schrödinger equations are mathematically identical; reduction of gravity to quantum mechanics or quantum mechanics to gravity; a new mixed Einstein–Schrödinger field equation; completion of operational metric–source attachment; derivation of common gravitational and inertial mass; construction of the full common upstream history object; completion of measurement-record or entanglement-compatibility machinery within this manuscript alone; a nonzero interacting quantum-gravity dynamics; unique realization across all possible targets, sources, potentials, states, or boundary conditions; derivation of a Wheeler–DeWitt equation; closure of every approach to quantum gravity. Those later constructive and certificate obligations are deliberately handed forward to Modules A–D and the integrated FEP-1.0 capstone. Conceptual Significance The manuscript replaces additive unification with common-origin unification. Gravity and quantum mechanics are not treated as independently complete physical systems that must somehow be joined after the fact. The admissible physical interior is prior to both. Einstein and Schrödinger dynamics are its distinct first-response projections, each carrying a specific role and neither exhausting the whole. This yields a disciplined hierarchy: general AASC machinery governs determinate construction; one admissible physical interior supplies the common physical basis; Einstein and Schrödinger dynamics arise as separate faithful projections; the joint-ledger condition governs their lawful compatibility; downstream modules construct and certify the complete shared realization. The result is the conceptual and theorem-level hinge of the completed ARC: it closes the Einstein–Schrödinger structural sequence and opens the constructive path toward full gravity–quantum certificate closure.
