A Structural Solution to the Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer Role-Compression Problem

Amos Jay Maley · 46 pages · Mathematical role compression

Read paperDownload PDFDOI

The manuscript supplies a structural role-compression closure for the elliptic curve, L-function, analytic order, arithmetic rank and bridge roles. Its claim-class locks leave classical Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer as a separate universal arithmetic bridge problem. This is not a proof of classical rank equality or its refined formula, Sha finiteness, new cases or rational-point construction.
