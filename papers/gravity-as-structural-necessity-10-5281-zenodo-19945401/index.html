Complete publisher description
An AASC Account of the GR-Type Physical Interior This paper develops the AASC account of gravity as a structural necessity of admissible physical existence. It does not begin by treating gravity as a primitive force, curvature field, metric ontology, or quantization target. Instead, it asks what architecture must be present for a physical interior to support determinate identity, admissible redescription, observable comparison, and lawful continuation. The paper identifies gravity, in the AASC sense, with the constraint role required for such an admissible physical interior: a standing surface; quotient identity for physical observables; admissible redescription; constraint closure; characteristic continuation; multiplier-type Hamiltonian presentation. Canonical general relativity is then treated as the paradigmatic realization of this role rather than as its primitive definition. Central result The principal result is the Gravity Necessity Theorem. Within the regular-stratum AER comparison class, an admissible physical interior satisfying quotient identity, observable completeness, separation, maximal same-scope observability, and presentation arbitrariness must instantiate a GR-type canonical constraint architecture. In plain terms: Gravity is the forced constraint role of admissible physical existence. The theorem does not claim that every conceivable formal system contains general relativity or Lorentzian geometry. It establishes that a system claiming to realize the same admissible physical interior cannot dispense with the constraint architecture that supports standing, quotient identity, observable descent, and admissible continuation. Local forcing chain The local mathematical argument is proved within the paper. Its dependency structure is: admissible physical standing yields a quotient architecture; observable descent and separation identify the physical redescription directions; maximal observable completeness eliminates hidden presymplectic remainders; the redescription distribution is identified with the characteristic distribution; characteristic equality forces coisotropic standing; the coisotropic normal form supplies local constraint generators; regularity gives first-class closure; presentation arbitrariness yields multiplier-type Hamiltonian evolution. The resulting local normal form contains: a coisotropic standing surface; characteristic redescription; local first-class constraints; presentation-parametrized Hamiltonian dynamics; representative evolution differing only by admissible redescription directions. These properties are derived from the AER interface and are not inserted as primitive gravitational assumptions. Fixed-domain closure The paper then closes the GR-type interior against same-scope alternatives. A genuine rival must first diverge through one of a finite set of loci: anchor or domain motion; quotient-locus motion; characteristic or redescription motion; bridge-neutral insertion later activated by a selector, reset, or second classifier. The first three alter the theorem object or its physical content. A bridge-neutral insertion has no same-scope physical effect unless it acquires authority through an additional selector, repair route, or undeclared authorizer. Such a move changes scope or violates the fixed-domain standing conditions. Consequently, within the fixed AER comparison class, no non-equivalent same-scope rival survives. The GR-type constraint architecture is the unique same-scope admissible realization of the observable-compatible quotient structure. This is a structural closure result, not a claim that all richer-scope theories are impossible. Curvature and canonical general relativity The paper does not deny the physical significance of curvature, metric variables, connection variables, ADM data, lapse, or shift. It assigns them a typed role within the architecture. Curvature is treated as valid tensorial content in a geometric projection of the underlying constraint structure. Coordinate, gauge, and presentation choices are treated as skin where they preserve the standing-bearing content. The proof of gravity necessity does not require metric geometry or curvature to be primitive. Canonical GR therefore appears as the standard geometric realization of a deeper admissibility-forced architecture: the constraint surface realizes standing; gauge and diffeomorphism structure realize admissible redescription; first-class closure realizes continuation; lapse and shift realize multiplier presentation freedom; geometric observables provide faithful physical projections. Projection architecture and quantization boundary For an independently supplied jointly realized gravity–quantum target, the paper constructs canonical role profiles from the target’s standing uses, histories, identities, and admissible continuations. It then separates three questions that are often conflated: whether canonical gravitational and quantum role profiles exist; whether supplied native gravitational and quantum projections realize those profiles; whether a direct gravitational-to-quantum map exists on the fixed physical target. A compatible direct map exists exactly when the gravitational projection does not identify two physical states that the quantum projection distinguishes. In fibre form, the required condition is: Fib(Π̄ᴳ) ⊆ Fib(Π̄Q). When this condition holds, the map is unique on the realized gravitational image. When it fails, a common source may still exist, but it does not supply a direct projection-to-projection map. The paper further shows why a primitive same-domain quantization claim encounters standing-bearing divergence unless representation, observables, constraint implementation, physical state selection, measure, boundary extraction, continuation, and record extraction are all properly typed. Effective, semiclassical, EFT, loop, spinfoam, string-like, and path-integral approaches remain admissible within their declared scopes; the obstruction concerns only an undeclared independent foundational authorizer for the same fixed target. Scope and non-claims The result is exact relative to the stated AER comparison class and does not claim: a derivation of Einstein’s field equations from no premises; derivation of numerical gravitational coupling constants; derivation of cosmological boundary conditions; that all modified-gravity theories are false; that curvature or Lorentzian geometry is unreal; that GR and QM are isomorphic mathematical structures; that every formal system, regardless of target, must contain GR-like geometry; that richer-scope theories cannot add new anchors, tensors, boundaries, or transport rules. The paper establishes a stronger claim than a mere interpretation of GR and a weaker claim than an unrestricted derivation of all gravitational physics from nothing. It proves that, within the fixed comparison class, an admissible physical interior cannot lack the AASC gravity role while remaining the same target. Role in the AASC programme This paper provides the gravity-facing bridge theorem of the AASC corpus. It isolates and proves the local constrained-Hamiltonian forcing chain, identifies canonical GR as its standard realization, closes same-scope gravitational rivals, and establishes the exact boundary between structural necessity, native realization, projection equivalence, and quantization. Its central contribution is the reclassification of gravity from a contingent physical sector to an admissibility-forced structural role: Gravity is not merely something an admissible physical interior contains. It is part of what makes that interior admissible at all. Place in the Completed Unification ARC The universal AASC kernel and the singleton-interior theorem of Unus Solus Possibilis Est are general machinery used throughout the wider research program. They are imported by this ARC but do not belong exclusively to it. Gravity as Structural Necessity is the first work specific to the gravity–quantum unification sequence. Its downstream position is: Gravity as Structural Necessity — establishes the necessary physical constraint role; Einstein Dynamics — derives the unique minimal first-response metric projection; Schrödinger Dynamics — derives the corresponding minimal Hamiltonian quantum projection; Structural Unification — proves that the gravitational and quantum descriptions are projections of one admissible physical interior; Module A — completes operational metric–source attachment. Module B — proves common mass–charge matching. Module C — constructs common upstream continuation and projection intertwining. Module D — establishes exact certificate-fibre exhaustion and unique lawful role occupancy. Complete Certificate Closure — integrates the full FEP-1.0 gravity–quantum realization certificate.
