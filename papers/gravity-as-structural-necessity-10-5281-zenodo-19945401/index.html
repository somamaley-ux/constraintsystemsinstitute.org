Gravity as Structural Necessity

Amos Jay Maley

Overview This paper presents a structural account of gravity within the AASC (Admissible Structure of Construction) framework. It argues that gravity is not a contingent dynamical sector, but the necessary constraint architecture required for the existence of an admissible physical interior.
