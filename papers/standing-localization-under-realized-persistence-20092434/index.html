Standing Localization under Realized Persistence

Amos Jay Maley · 18 pages · Fixed-domain continuation and persistence foundations

Read paperDownload PDFDOI

The manuscript derives bounded standing-localization equivalence from realized persistence with lawful repair semantics. Perturbation plus nonzero repair capacity is one stated route to those semantics. Lawful continuation-level correction is distinguished from prohibited restoration of a standing-failed occurrence as the same act. Localization concerns a bounded identity and repair role locus; it does not establish a physical body, spacetime region, biochemical repair or biological life.
