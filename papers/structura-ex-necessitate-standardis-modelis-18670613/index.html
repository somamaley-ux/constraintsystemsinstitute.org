The manuscript gives Standard-Model bookkeeping closure relative to its declared load and admissibility class. Its full renormalizable witness ledger retains couplings, Higgs and flavor data, and theta-bar; restricted low-energy descriptions collapse fewer combinations. It does not establish uniqueness over all quantum field theories or derive numerical parameters. Richer loads and certified witnesses remain scope changes.
