L2 The Unclosable Contingent Reality

Amos Jay Maley · 31 January 2026 · 3 pages

Read paperDownload PDFPublication records

The January classification note treats contingent particulars as admissible, witnessable facts not derivable from admissibility constraints alone. It distinguishes that derivational limit from a mechanism of instance selection and does not offer a probabilistic or metaphysical explanation of particulars.
