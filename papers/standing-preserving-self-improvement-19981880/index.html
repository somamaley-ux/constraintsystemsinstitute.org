Standing-Preserving Self-Improvement

Amos Jay Maley · 36 pages · Standing and agent governance

Read paperDownload PDFDOI

The manuscript classifies same-scope capability improvement by standing-envelope contraction, governance strengthening and continued-reference preservation. Composing improvements cannot bootstrap standing within that scope; new evidence and fresh scopes remain available. Its Standing-Preserving Update Test is necessary diagnostic eligibility, rather than certification of correctness, safety, alignment or consciousness.
