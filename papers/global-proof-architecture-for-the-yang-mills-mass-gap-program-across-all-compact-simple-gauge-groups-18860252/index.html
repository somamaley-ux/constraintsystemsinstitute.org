This short architecture paper maps the SU(2), SU(N), classical and exceptional Yang-Mills families onto a shared Lane-C, Lane-A and Route-1 organization with family-specific entrance gates. It is a navigational series map, adding no lemmas or new mathematical claims. Exact imported releases are unspecified, and its local-sector organization does not settle nonlocal line or surface sectors.
