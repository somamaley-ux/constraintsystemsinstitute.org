Persistence-Regime Realization under AASC

Amos Jay Maley · 22 pages · Fixed-domain continuation and persistence foundations

Read paperDownload PDFDOI

The manuscript induces a persistence-regime equivalence class from irreversible standing-bearing continuation in its fixed role-anchor target class. Realization here means consequence-retaining lineage under lawful transport, quotient stability and fail-closed standing. Role fixation alone is insufficient without the additional irreversible-continuation conditions. The result preserves the inherited fixation conditions and distinguishes realization from trace succession, concrete instantiation, physical embodiment and biological life.
