It connects massless QCD flavor structure, axial anomalies, vacuum realization, Goldstone modes, isospin, hadronic flavor, custodial symmetry, effective-field-theory limits, and heavy-quark symmetry without collapsing classical invariance, quantum anomaly, vacuum realization, explicit deformation, approximation, topology, or renormalization into a single status label. The declared domain contains eighteen atomic records organized across six families. Each record receives an eleven-field normal form and a source-identified evidence certificate. The conventional quantum-field-theoretic layer constructs and classifies: the faithful massless-QCD chiral action and center quotient; nonsinglet and singlet Ward identities; the discrete axial residue and ABJ anomaly; conditional vacuum stabilizers and Goldstone realization; anomaly matching and Wess–Zumino–Witten transport; exact vector-flavor, isospin, and hadronic SU(3)SU(3) limits; chiral spurion counting and controlled deformation order; scalar, gauge-Higgs, and matter-coupled custodial structure; custodial diagnostics and symmetry-breaking ancestry; heavy-field decomposition and the leading HQET expansion; heavy-quark spin/flavor symmetry and current matching; operator-level corrections and heavy-hadron occupancy. The paper maintains a strict distinction between exact symmetries, approximate symmetries, anomalous currents, vacuum-realized symmetries, effective symmetries, transport relations, diagnostics, and target updates. The QCD chiral vacuum witness is registered as a conventional physics input; its existence and numerical value are not derived within this work. Likewise, low-energy constants, Wilson coefficients, hadronic matrix elements, and empirical scale comparisons remain conventional inputs where explicitly stated. The result closes the declared SYM-6 flavor, chiral, custodial, and infrared architecture. It exports a custodial packet to SYM-8, an effective-theory and renormalization-group transport packet to SYM-10, and an anomaly/infrared packet to the all-arc capstone. Full global-form, line-operator, monopole, defect, finite-temperature, and generalized-symmetry closure remains outside the present theorem boundary.
