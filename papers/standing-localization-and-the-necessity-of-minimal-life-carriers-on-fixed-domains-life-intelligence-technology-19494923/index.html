Standing Localization and the Necessity of Minimal Life Carriers on Fixed Domains (Life, Intelligence, Technology)

Amos Jay Maley · 17 pages · Standing localization and life carriers

Read paperDownload PDFDOI

At fixed finite resolution and under finite fixation time, the manuscript derives an inclusion-minimal bounded carrier with boundary, identity-memory and local-repair roles. It calls this defined structural carrier minimal life. The claim is not a biochemical or taxonomic classification of all life. Intelligence is conditional, and the technology discussion concerns the fixed-scope interface budget.
