Early Metric-Interior Transport Closure

Amos Jay Maley · 47 pages · Metric-envelope and Higgs carrier-order reading pair

Read paperDownload PDFDOI

The manuscript classifies support for later coherent witness families in a fixed admissible realized interior. If independent local trajectory continuation is insufficient, its necessity theorem allows early transport/closure, a declared boundary anchor or a declared richer scope; it also identifies forbidden support routes. Only the same-scope corollary with those alternatives excluded forces an early transport/closure certificate. The inflationary role is a declared metric-field-vacuum projection of that structural certificate. Closure stress means successor-neighborhood thinness, not stress-energy. The result neither selects an inflation model nor derives empirical inflation parameters, and explicitly permits richer-scope alternatives.
