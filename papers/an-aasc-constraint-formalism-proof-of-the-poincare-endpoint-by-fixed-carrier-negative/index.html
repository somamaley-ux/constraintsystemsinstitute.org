An AASC Constraint-Formalism Proof of the Poincare Endpoint by Fixed-Carrier Negative-Branch Exclusion

Amos Jay Maley

Overview This deposit presents An AASC Constraint-Formalism Proof of the Poincaré Endpoint by Fixed-Carrier Negative-Branch Exclusion . The manuscript gives a mathematical proof in the Admissibility And Standing Constraint (AASC) formalism. It is not a Ricci-flow proof, a surgery proof, a recognition algorithm proof, or an imported corollary of the Hamilton-Perelman solution.
