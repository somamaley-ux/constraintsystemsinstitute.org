Amos Jay Maley · 39 pages

Read paperDownload PDFExact edition DOIWork DOI · all versions

Classifies failures of complete realization for an independently specified task: incorrect or missing results, lost distinctions, misrepresented safe continuations and failed certificate obligations. Every defect has an original-task witness.

What this edition establishes

Complete original outcomes, including definedness, give a canonical quotient. A true conclusion can still lack protocol-required support; certificate obligations are separate from the truth of the conclusion.

For partial deterministic updates the paper characterizes the quotient preserving all finite observations, and distinguishes determination by a full profile from expression by finitely many observations. Grounded authorization has an exact conservative-extension criterion.

The results specify failures that survive faithful redescription. A unique output or occupant requires its own additional evidence; fixed-domain exhaustion does not silently enlarge the original task or its declared uses.
