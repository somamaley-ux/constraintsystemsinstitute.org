About this paper and citation
Imports
Reuses the 20 September Structure profile/interior results with short local proofs. Ref.6 names kernel v49; ref.7 names AMetric editorial reference. ATS is a comparison and is explicitly not assumed.
Supplies downstream
Supplies the finite-use compositional foundation and candidate readout criterion imported by ATS. ATS cites the 20 September composition reference rather than this preferred 21 September publication reference.
Scope and limits
Arbitrary semantic coordinates are not automatically a finite implementation. Candidate-set constant readout, single occupant, and unique decomposition are separate results with separate assumptions. Retaining a failure history does not itself force every later endpoint negative; the edge 0-to-0 condition is additional.
