The Higgs Bridge and the Metric Envelope

Amos Jay Maley · 39 pages · Metric-envelope and Higgs carrier-order reading pair

Read paperDownload PDFDOI

The manuscript constructs a carrier-order witness in its fixed Higgs/electroweak/gravity comparison scope. The Higgs bridge/fixation carrier is standing-subordinate to the metric-envelope carrier imported from early metric-interior closure. A faithful positive closure-scale projection displays the resulting strict order as a ratio between zero and one; this requires the stated projection conditions. Monotone reparameterization preserves order while generally changing ratios. The result does not derive a numerical electroweak/Planck ratio, finite suppression class, unique projection or Higgs-sector parameters. Certificate assembly is a manuscript construction, not independent formal verification.
