Standing–Admissibility Identity Closure (Equivalence)

Amos Jay Maley · 12 pages · Fixed-domain continuation and persistence foundations

Read paperDownload PDFDOI

The manuscript states a conditional identity result for two binary classifiers on a fixed evaluated-state domain. Its explicit substrate includes lawful continuation, an AMetric no-selector boundary, standing conservation and irrecoverability on retained lineages. It separates pointwise classifier identity from collapse of a remaining governance-role distinction. It does not derive a lower kernel, assert global minimality or govern cross-domain transport.
