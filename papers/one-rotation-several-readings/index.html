One Rotation, Several Readings

Persistence, sector exchange, and the identifiability of orbital angular momentum

Amos Jay Maley · 25 pages

Read paperDownload PDF

One conserved rotation charge need not determine internal orbital readings. An exact Wilson-path comparison on one Gauss-constrained preparation shows which distinctions survive and which require another probe.

What the rotation charge leaves open

The paper separates the identity of a physical source, the existence of its conserved rotation response, and the recoverability of an internal reading. It derives sector torque balance with boundary flux and gives examples where complete aggregate readings leave a marked internal reading unresolved.

Two paths on the same preparation

On a Maxwell–Schrödinger preparation, a straight Wilson link and a finite staple give different signed orbital moments. Reflected staples agree on that moment but differ when a longitudinal weight is retained. Co-transporting the entire probe is a gauge redescription; changing the path alone can change what is measured.

Scope

For the physical proton, the paper identifies the matched flavor-singlet potential angular momentum that would separate two specified gluon assignments. Its value or vanishing remains open. The paper does not compute a new proton spin fraction.
