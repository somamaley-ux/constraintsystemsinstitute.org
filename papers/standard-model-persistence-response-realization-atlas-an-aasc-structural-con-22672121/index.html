This figure is a structural dependency atlas for the Standard Model in the AASC constraint formalism. It shows how a single nondegenerate target and a common persistence bearer resolve into differentiated carrier roles and then into physical projection. The arrows are logical dependence, not a temporal creation sequence. There is no backflow from realized particles to the kernel. Reading order: Premetric constraint architecture. Gravity enters here as a structural necessity that licenses localization, causal comparison, and field propagation. It is not a Standard Model carrier. Common distributed persistence bearer. Local standing, identity, comparison, and transport are fixed before particle names. Joint structural response. Transport/comparison, handed representation, charge/gauge response, and order-deformation mediation resolve roles of the same persistence source. Derived carrier roles.  Standing response and sector differentiation. Three generation classes are split by rooted Higgs-response differentiation  Bosonic charge, color, and weak responses remain jointly compatible. Vacuum fixation, polarization, resonance readiness, confinement boundary, and metric/Einstein response appear only after those roles are fixed. Common physical realization/projection. The ordinary light-generation roster, photon,, confined color projection, and physical Higgs resonance are displayed as projections of the occupied source, not as independently assembled sectors.
