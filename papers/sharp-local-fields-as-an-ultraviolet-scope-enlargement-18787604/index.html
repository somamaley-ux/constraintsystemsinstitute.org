Sharp Local Fields as an Ultraviolet Scope Enlargement

The manuscript distinguishes a gap on a bounded fixed-positive-resolution vacuum algebra from the additional ultraviolet regularity needed for sharp point fields and larger sectors. A small-algebra gap does not establish a gap on an enlarged algebra; restriction runs in the safe direction. Sharp-field completion requires additional inputs.
