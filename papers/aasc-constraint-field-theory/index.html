AASC Constraint Field Theory

Structural Unification in an Admissible Interior
Homogeneous Einstein–Schrödinger Realizations at Fixed Spatial Cutoff

Amos Jay Maley · 84 pages

Read paperDownload PDFPhilPapers record

What must a physical domain retain for its tests and continuations to remain faithful? The paper constructs a minimal carrier for exact replay and tests it in fixed-spatial-cutoff Einstein–Schrödinger models.

What a constraint field carries

The paper identifies histories only when they agree on the outcomes, failures and continuations of the declared tests. On a nonflat Klein–Gordon branch, native tests retain the signed field germ, while compact metric tests on the same domain retain its square.

What geometry and quantum tests determine together

In one homogeneous model, ray tests determine the Hamiltonian up to a scalar function of scale. The pressure reading determines its derivative, and one energy or constraint reading fixes the remaining constant. Other paired preparations have identical initial energy laws but different metric accelerations.

Scope

These are results for the stated source models, tests and fixed spatial cutoff. They neither derive quantum positivity from the AASC Kernel alone nor quantize the metric or construct a continuum interacting field theory.
