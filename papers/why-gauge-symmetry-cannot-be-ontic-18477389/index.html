Why Gauge Symmetry Cannot Be Ontic

Amos Jay Maley · 3 pages · Gauge representation and ontology

Read paperDownload PDFDOI

Using stated criteria of representation independence, additional constraint imposition and non-redundancy, the manuscript argues for a representational interpretation of gauge symmetry under locality and internal consistency. Its conclusion is a philosophical eliminative argument, preserving the usefulness of gauge descriptions. It is not presented as independently verified ontology or a blanket conclusion over other research.
