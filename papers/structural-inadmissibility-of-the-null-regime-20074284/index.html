The Structural Inadmissibility of the Null Regime

Amos Jay Maley · 33 pages · Fixed-domain continuation and persistence foundations

Read paperDownload PDFDOI

The manuscript excludes total AASC vacuity as an admissible same-type fixed-domain closure regime. It distinguishes that target from formal empty objects, an all-rejecting classifier, raw absence and mute non-claims. Admissibility-relevant use of an empty-extension classification still incurs evaluability obligations. The result is a structural closure claim; it does not establish concrete instantiation, cosmological genesis or an unrestricted rejection of nullist theses.
