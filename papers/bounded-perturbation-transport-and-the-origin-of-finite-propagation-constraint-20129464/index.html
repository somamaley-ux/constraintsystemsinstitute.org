Bounded Perturbation Transport and the Origin of Finite-Propagation Constraint

Amos Jay Maley · 14 pages · Realized Physics Arc - Paper 5

Read paperDownload PDFDOI

The manuscript argues that admissible standing-relevant perturbation transport must preserve boundary witnesses and quotient comparison through admitted continuation paths. It establishes witnessed transport reach, including admitted global boundary witnesses. It does not derive finite physical speed, temporal causation, light cones or a field propagation law; those require additional metric, temporal and dynamical structure.
