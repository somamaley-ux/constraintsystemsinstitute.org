Primitive Necessity, Exact-Three Occupation, and Nonpromotion under Enrichment This paper establishes an open-generation theorem for the cardinality of a primitive generation architecture. The result is derived before empirical family matching, numerical mass values, mixing matrices, or preferred embedded substructures are introduced. The original primitive task is specified with multiplicity open and requires the joint realization of: inherited representation, charge, and weak-chiral compatibility; compatible mass-response slots; direct comparison of generation occurrences; boundary preparation, propagation, and endpoint reconstruction; nonvacuous holonomy or phase capacity; selector independence; joint realization of the complete duty package. The central result is: Complete primitive generation standing at cardinality n ⇔ n = 3. This is a theorem about primitive role occupancy within the declared open-generation domain. It is not an empirical count of observed families or a dimension count of a downstream matrix. Primitive Capacity and the Exact-Three Endpoint The comparison architecture is complete on the candidate occurrence set and is considered modulo vertex-local rephasing. The paper proves that non-removable comparison capacity first appears on a triple of occurrences: fewer than three positions have only removable gradient-like comparison data; every minimal live support is a triangle; every larger capacity-bearing architecture contains a proper live three-position support; the minimal bare architecture has one isomorphism type, the complete three-vertex graph; the associated primitive comparison quotient has rank one. The rank-one residue is therefore a consequence of the derived three-position architecture. It is not inserted as an independent premise and does not determine the cardinality by itself. The argument distinguishes ordinary non-removable capacity from reversal-sensitive capacity. A particular realized parameter point may be CP-conserving while the architecture retains the capacity required by the primitive task. Architectural capacity and the status of an individual physical parameter point are separate questions. Joint Realization of Primitive Duties A nonzero comparison residue alone is not sufficient. The primitive work must be realized jointly. The paper constructs a common receiving packet that simultaneously supports: the inherited matter and charge representations; weak-chiral and bridge compatibility; charged-response slots; direct comparison and phase quotient data; coherent boundary preparation and endpoint readout; nonvacuous holonomy; selector-independent role formation; joint compatibility and failure records. The construction is uniform over the admitted finite phase classes. It uses one common parameter packet for the declared primitive duties rather than proving each duty separately in incompatible realizations. This establishes both directions of the endpoint: no fewer than three positions can perform the complete primitive work; a three-position architecture can perform it jointly. The count is therefore a constructional minimum and a forced endpoint, not a fitted parameter or an observed multiplicity. Role Formation and Complete Occupation The primitive role template is formed from the minimal adequate architecture before candidate residues, observed families, or downstream matching are considered. For a live candidate architecture, all compatible minimal preassignments are retained. Their live union defines the primitive occupant set. Complete occupation requires: a compatible assignment from the derived role template; coverage of the entire live primitive union; no omitted live occupant; no added primitive role; one joint physical receipt for the complete assignment; lawful transport under role-incidence permutations. The paper proves: ClosedGenCertₚ₀(n) ⇔ n = 3. At the endpoint, the three primitive positions are homogeneous occurrences of one derived architecture. They are not three different unary work-types, and the theorem does not depend on choosing one preferred embedded triangle. Alternative role assignments form one lawful role-incidence orbit up to permutation. This gives uniqueness of the compatible role occupation at the architectural level without claiming that all physical parameter values, phase assignments, or microscopic realizations are identical. Nonpromotion under Enrichment A central theorem excludes primitive promotion by downstream enlargement. Additional responses, interactions, matrix entries, numerical values, executions, or physical inventories may carry legitimate standing within an enriched task. They do not acquire an additional primitive generation role for the unchanged primitive work merely by being present. For an enlarged architecture with more than three positions, one of two things occurs: it fails one of the original primitive duties; or it contains a proper three-position support that already realizes the complete primitive task. In the second case, the larger architecture is not primitive-minimal. It may represent a richer physical or mathematical system, but its additional positions are not forced primitive roles for the original generation problem. This distinction is essential: a primitive carrier is not the same as a downstream physical role carrier; primitive architecture is not the same as field-copy count; primitive role occupancy is not the same as matrix dimension; primitive cardinality is not selected by masses, labels, or fitted textures. Count Descent without a Preferred Witness The paper also gives a relational receiving theorem. An enriched object may receive one or more licensed primitive constructions without containing a canonically selected embedded triangle. If the receipt relation is nonempty, every received primitive construction has the same count: Nₚᵣᵢₘ = 3. Thus the primitive cardinality descends through the receiving relation without requiring: a preferred embedded triangle; a selector for one witness; a distinguished orientation; a unique phase representative; or a function choosing one primitive receipt from all admissible receipts. Further original answers descend only when they are constant on the actual receipt fibres. Primitive count has this property automatically; phase values, detailed histories, or other enriched outputs may not. Grounded Derived Authority The paper proves that grounded derived constructions preserve the original primitive qualification closure when every added macro expands into an explicit derivation from the original rules. This permits useful downstream constructions while excluding claims of new primitive authority arising solely from: later-stage placement; reclassification; a derived operator name; a circular certificate; or a macro whose premises already contain the conclusion’s only support. New independent premises may change the enriched task, but they require their own warrant. They do not retroactively alter the primitive count of the original construction. Scope and Nonclaims The theorem is established in an open-generation arena in which the primitive occurrence multiplicity is not fixed in advance. It does not claim: that the observed number of fermion families is itself an empirical proof of the theorem; that masses, mixing angles, CKM or PMNS entries, oscillation data, anomaly data, or fitted textures select the count; that the theorem derives every downstream Standard Model parameter; that the primitive carrier is identical to every physical field or enriched receiver; that a larger enriched theory is impossible; that every larger carrier lacks legitimate physical roles; that the theorem identifies a unique microscopic realization; that a preferred embedded triangle or phase representative must exist; that the paper replaces the domain-specific receiving proof required by a downstream application. A fourth generation or larger architecture would need to demonstrate a same-scope primitive role-occupancy certificate for the unchanged duty package. Merely adding downstream structure is not such a certificate. Role in the AASC Programme This paper supplies the generation-cardinality endpoint of the AASC carrier and role-occupancy programme: kernel necessity → primitive duty package → non-removable comparison capacity → joint three-position realization → forced role template → complete occupation → nonpromotion under enrichment. Its conclusion is structural and constructional: The complete primitive generation work has exactly three primitive role positions, and downstream enrichment cannot add further primitive authority for that unchanged work. The result provides a governance-level generation endpoint for receiving constructions while preserving the distinction between primitive role occupancy, enriched physical content, and the detailed values realized within a particular physical system.
