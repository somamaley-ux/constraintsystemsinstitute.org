The Anthropic Collapse Theorem

Amos Jay Maley · 24 pages · Constants and Ensemble Arc - Paper 5

Read paperDownload PDFDOI

The manuscript distinguishes primitive anthropic explanation from conditional inference within a supplied ensemble. It argues that observer conditioning presupposes a parameter space, measure, observer predicate, witness ledger and comparison transport. The paired survival theorem permits posterior filtering and evidential updating inside independently licensed ensembles; the result does not refute all anthropic models.
