Constraint Architectures for Coherent Evaluability (Mind Paper)

Amos Jay Maley

Overview This paper develops a formal constraint architecture for coherent evaluability in fixed-domain admissible evaluation systems. Rather than presenting a permissive taxonomy of evaluative regimes, the framework treats coherent evaluability as a structurally constrained phenomenon.
