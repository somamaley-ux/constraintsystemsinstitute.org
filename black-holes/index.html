Black Holes

The limits of spacetime. Fresh origins without inheritance.

A black-hole geometry is one thing. The conditions that let its physical content remain identifiable are another. This five-paper arc follows that distinction from exterior reconstruction to the AMetric endpoint, and from there to a fresh domain governed by its own constraints.

00 / Fix the physical question

A horizon is not
a reconstruction map.

General relativity supplies black-hole geometries within its declared regimes. This arc preserves that role. Its additional question is whether a description can keep identifying the same physical reference through later exterior witnesses.

Here, standing means more than an observer knowing something: the declared structure supports identifying the target again and using it consistently. A scope fixes which references, witnesses and continuations belong to that reconstruction problem.

The kernel is not an optional layer added to such a construction. Once the target is non-degenerate and determinate, admissibility, standing, reference and irreversibility govern it. The later black-hole results then depend on the particular reconstruction and endpoint conditions established in the arc.

The technical distinction

Paper 0 separates a classical event horizon from a same-scope, standing-preserving exterior reconstruction object. The latter fixes an exterior witness algebra, a reference quotient, a continuation relation and explicit transport status. The familiar Kerr-Newman mass, charge and angular-momentum ledger is an example of exterior role content, not a stationarity or no-hair premise for the whole theorem.

Singularities are treated as diagnostics of exhausted metric articulation. Thermodynamic and information-theoretic ledgers retain their proper roles, but do not acquire reconstruction force without an appropriate witness map. The arc does not compute a Hawking spectrum, a Page curve or interior microdynamics.

01 / The exterior obstruction

A missing witness cannot
be filled by a label.

Suppose some previously supported references become dependent on a permanently isolated horizon interior. The exterior reconstruction problem still asks for those references to remain identifiable. What supplies their later exterior witness?

Paper I distinguishes a genuine leakage or reconstruction route from the assertion that the information is stored somewhere. A boundary, radiation, holographic or remnant map can resolve the burden when it actually re-fixes the reference in the declared scope.

Without such a route, the assignment is undefined. Assigning a convenient label does not turn it into a justified reconstruction. If no admissible exterior successor remains, the result is exhaustion of that reconstruction, not successful standing-preserving permanence.

The partial-domain rule and the non-claim

The illustration instantiates R = {r1, r2, r3, r4} and Rhid = {r3, r4}. Under the paper's toy rule, dom(τ) = R when leakage or a certificate supplies the required witnesses; otherwise dom(τ) = R ∖ Rhid. This is not a model of radiation dynamics, an information-capacity calculation or a numerical prediction.

The full theorem is scope-locked. A genuine certificate defeats the uncertified antecedent and is an allowed resolution. Larger-scope retention can also be meaningful, but is not automatically recovery in the original exterior scope. Paper I does not yet conclude that the interior is residual, AMetric or universe-generating.

Licensed roles

Description and role support agree.

The declared domain supports reference, comparison and reuse. Metric, source and record descriptions operate within that governed scope.

A black-hole horizon is a structure within such a licensed projection, not a new source of its licensing.

These are structural cases, not time steps, radial positions or measured degrees of standing. Standing remains bivalent.

Why a pattern can remain

Residual coherence requires a traceable earlier role, retained organization, a non-null representation and failure of full role licensing. It is not a new substance, a hidden energy store or a small amount of standing.

Why “deeper” changes meaning

Governance-depth concerns which residual continuations remain supported. When metric-comparison successor support is exhausted, even “deeper inside” loses that residual metric basis. This is not a claim about an infalling observer's experience or a calculated radius.

Paper II / Residual coherence and governance-depth
The residual-coherence conditions

ResCohrD(x) is role-indexed: it requires pre-break ancestry in role r, standing loss, no certificate or new governance, non-null post-break representation, retained organization and failure of full role licensing. Governance-depth is ordered by inclusion of residual successor-support classes, not by a universal scalar ranking. The exact metric-depth failure condition is SuccresD(x, Metric) = ∅. That condition alone is not the complete AMetric endpoint theorem: Paper III must still establish its remaining target coordinates and route closures.

What has to be established before the endpoint result applies?

Theorem 5.9 requires a nonempty post-subtraction residue, undefined old-scope standing transport, exhausted residual metric successor support, no independent residue-level re-governance before exhaustion, closed endpoint routes and unavailable admitted nullity. A surviving witness resolves its burden; surviving metric support means this endpoint target has not yet been established; a separately governed richer scope changes the target.

The normal-form proof independently fixes metric, temporal, causal, operator, selector, transport, valuation, repair, target-gating and self-authorization coordinates, with the null route closed. Theorem 6.2 then gives B☆A ≠ B0A and equal complete AMetric role profiles. It supplies no transfer of energy, information, laws, records or identity, and no populated successor follows from homology alone.

04 / The counter-boundary capstone

Fresh governance.
Not inherited contents.

Paper IV adds a result that the earlier papers do not claim. Starting from the completed endpoint and homology conditions, it closes the remaining counter-boundary alternatives. The surviving status is a fresh internal governed domain, unique up to governance equivalence.

“Fresh” matters. The domain does not receive the earlier domain's energy, information, clock, history, laws or numerical constants. Its minimal constraint architecture recurs because non-degenerate domainhood requires it, not because a package travels through the boundary.

For the certified endpoint target, this is a forced status-class result. It is not a selected, populated universe with a calculated history. Realized physical content and Standard Model structure require their own further target conditions.

What recurs, and what does not cross

Minimal role architecture

Admissibility, standing, reference and irreversibility; independent AMetric licensing.

Parent-scope contents

No transferred energy, information, metric, law token, record, clock or parameter value.

Further physical specialization

Additional physical structure belongs to the fresh domain's own declared and supported target.

The surviving governance-equivalence class

Under the certified endpoint antecedents and completed route closure, the capstone establishes fresh internal governed-domain status. No parent content is needed or permitted to cross.

Paper IV / The Counter-Boundary Universe Theorem
Status-existence, physical realization and the scope of the conclusion

Theorem 6.2 uses antecedents A1-A9 and the Completed Closure Register Lemma. Its result is exactly one admissible counter-boundary status-class up to ≡gov,CB, represented by a token-distinct domain instantiating its own kernel and AMetric licensing profile. The route analysis, not an animation or an assumed successor, carries the existence and uniqueness claim.

The manuscript distinguishes this status-existence from populated existence, token selection and inherited cosmography. No tunneling, bounce, causal production or transfer mechanism is derived. Nor does an astronomical black-hole label by itself establish all endpoint antecedents. A richer third-domain theory can introduce other comparison structures, but is then a different governance scope.
