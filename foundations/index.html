Late 2025 / My starting question

The spacetime and branching pictures mattered to me because the theories behind them agree so well with experiment. Whatever deeper understanding I reached, it had to preserve what they already got right.

Einstein’s relativity brings space and time into one description, with no single “now” shared by the whole universe. In the block picture, the events of my life belong to that spacetime history.

Quantum mechanics presented a different picture. Schrödinger’s equation contains no collapse mechanism. When that evolution is applied to the system, apparatus and observer together, the quantum state retains the different outcome records. Many Worlds takes those branches as physically real, each containing an observer with a definite experience.

I could not reconcile a single complete history with this multiplicity of realized histories as complete accounts of the same reality. Both grew out of mathematics I had strong experimental reasons to take seriously. Yet I still had to account for the life I was experiencing.

That was what drove me to search for something deeper and more fundamental. What could both descriptions depend on? What would have to remain true through either account of the universe?

I started with the one thing I could examine from the inside: my own experience—“me.”

What the two pictures mean

The spacetime drawing represents an ordered history. Relativity supplies no preferred universal present sweeping through it. Our highlight is a reading aid, not a moving cosmic “now.” The block interpretation is a philosophical reading of spacetime; the diagram does not establish that interpretation or refute it. Einstein Online: spacetime and relative simultaneity.

The branching drawing is a schematic measurement interaction in an Everett-style description: the quantum state includes correlated observer and outcome records. It is not branching caused by an ordinary human decision. Each represented observer has a definite record; first-person experience by itself is not a refutation of Everett. A request for one uniquely selected continuation is an additional question whose justification must be made explicit. Hugh Everett III, “Relative State” Formulation of Quantum Mechanics (1957).

The conflict I recall concerns treating one classical history, or multiple realized histories, as the complete account of reality. A block conception of time does not by itself exclude Everettian branching. Wallace and Timpson: quantum mechanics on spacetime.

The experimental motivation comes from the tested successes of the underlying theories; it does not uniquely establish either interpretation. Experimental tests of relativity; Molecular interference tests of quantum superposition.

These pictures introduce the historical question that motivated AASC. They are not a proof of its later results. The interactive highlight changes which record you inspect; it neither changes that record nor identifies a physically preferred observer.

A light-second is a unit of distance: how far light travels in one second. The diagram uses time × the speed of light on its time axis. Changing the selected frame does not accelerate the watch or replay its history.

For a frame moving right at 60% of light speed, the time gap is 5.00 seconds and the position gap is −3.00 light-seconds. The watch still records 4.00 seconds, and the spacetime interval is still 16.00 light-seconds². These are two coordinate descriptions of the same pair of events.

The unchanged interval is a real example of an invariant. It still uses a metric: a rule that combines the time and position coordinates.

What must be settled before we use that description?

The transformation, the units, and what this example shows

This is special relativity in flat spacetime with one spatial coordinate. The watch follows an inertial worldline. In its rest frame, A = (ct, x) = (0, 0) and B = (4, 0), with both coordinates in light-seconds. The chosen frame moves at v = βc relative to the watch; the two frame origins coincide at A.

ct′ = γ(ct − βx),   x′ = γ(x − βct),   γ = 1 / √(1 − β²).

Using the time-positive sign convention, the squared interval is (Δct′)² − (Δx′)² = 16 light-seconds². Its square root divided by c is the proper elapsed time, 4 seconds, along this inertial watch's path. The drawing holds the rest-frame event positions fixed and maps the moving frame's coordinate grid back onto that drawing with the inverse transformation.

4.00² − 0.00² = 16.00 light-seconds².

This exhibits a Lorentz invariant inside an already specified spacetime description. It does not establish that the Lorentz metric is the deepest possible invariant or derive the foundations from this example.

A deliberate way of working

AASC took shape
between sleep and waking.

I had reached Lorentz invariance, but I was still searching for something more fundamental. What did even a metric description depend on?

I deliberately used the hypnopompic state—the transition from sleep to wakefulness—to explore those questions.

Before going to sleep, I would build mental models of the universe and identify the points I could not resolve. On waking, I would return to those questions in the in-between state, when I found my thinking most creative. I made this a working practice.

Over the space of a couple of weeks, I developed the core of AASC through this same process: the AMetric boundary, fixation, bivalence and uniqueness.

We can begin exploring those ideas with the AMetric boundary: when the readings change, what keeps them connected to the same thing?

My recollection of how AASC began in late 2025.

The hypnopompic state: sleep, waking and creative insight

Hypnopompic describes the transition from sleep to wakefulness; hypnagogic describes the transition into sleep. My practice deliberately focused on the waking transition.

The sleep-state terminology

Dalí documented his “slumber with a key” in Fifty Secrets of Magic Craftsmanship (1948), an example of deliberately interrupting sleep onset. Poincaré described ideas coming to him in bed in the morning or evening, and emphasized the conscious preparation and verification surrounding an insight.

The step toward the AMetric boundary

A description needs a definite
thing to be a description of.

A time, a distance or a label cannot settle its own connection to that thing merely by being written down. We have to establish what the description concerns and why its use is justified.

This is the question the AMetric boundary makes precise. “Before measurement” means a requirement measurement depends on. It does not name an earlier moment or a hidden place in the universe.

What this shows—and what the formal paper establishes

The display keeps the event identities and order supplied by the example. Hiding coordinates does not remove the physical metric or construct an equality-only input. It helps expose the distinction between the target, a representation of it, and the grounds that connect them.

The revised AMetric paper asks which distinctions and answers are supported by the complete authoritative input. A proposed choice must be eligible and remain fixed under every lawful transformation that leaves its supplied input unchanged (Theorem 4.6). Full arbitrary relabeling requires the equality-only premise; absence of a preferred individual alone does not erase other structure (§3.3 and §6).

The fibre test and constructed Axiom 6 concern what a description can establish and the order of qualification (Theorems 7.2 and 7.5). A warranted new record, test or construction can supply additional information. The boundary is not a physical transmission channel, and absence of a metric alone does not establish non-action.

04 / From the question to the foundations

Still about you.
Then about anything definite.

I began by asking how my own life fits into physics. That grew into a question about every definite person, object and event: what must a description preserve to remain an account of that same thing?

The Kernel gives four connected roles precise names. Their work is already present in the questions we have been asking.

Reference

What are we talking about?

This person. This experience. These particular events. A new description must keep its connection to its subject.

Standing

Why does this account count?

Writing a possible description is a beginning. It must meet the requirements for the particular claim it makes.

Irreversibility

Can a later story change what happened?

New evidence can correct our account of an event. A later event does not rewrite the original one.

Admissibility

Does the account hold together?

It must satisfy the relevant conditions together and preserve them through the uses it claims to support.

The person and the events do not acquire these requirements because someone notices them. In the Kernel argument, they are necessary for anything definite, including what goes unobserved.

How the personal starting point becomes a general result

The origin story motivates the investigation. The Kernel paper's derivation proceeds from determinate targets, assessment and use, historical fixation, and answer accountability (Definitions 2.1–2.3, Proposition 2.4 and Theorem 3.1). Consciousness is not introduced as an extra physical force or as a premise needed to govern other objects.

Its conservation results concern independently warranted answers to the same complete determinate question about the same bearer (Theorems 6.2–6.3). Agreement follows under those conditions; agreement alone does not establish that two accounts concern the same thing. Evidence about an unchanged occurrence is distinguished from a new occurrence or a changed construction (Example 3.2 and Lemma 3.3).

Read the Kernel paper
Why a complete account needs more than one yes-or-no answer

An account can answer one question correctly while leaving out something needed for another. The revised Structure paper therefore studies the full information required for the intended questions and uses, including whether a requested operation is defined. One qualification answer is binary; the complete account need not reduce to that single bit (Theorems 5.5–5.8).

It then constructs the greatest set that continues to meet the independently specified requirements throughout the permitted uses. The term admissible interior names that complete set. The requirements and permitted uses are specified before the set is found (Theorem 6.3 and §8).

Read The Structure of Admissibility
Does a unique complete answer mean only one possibility remains?

Not automatically. A complete answer includes every possibility that meets all the specified requirements. There can be one uniquely complete set with several members—or no members. Omitting a qualifying possibility and including an unqualified one are different ways to give an incomplete or incorrect answer.

Unus Solus Possibilis Est constructs the greatest sound interior from independent qualification, warrants and permitted continuations (§§2–3). A unique individual occupant requires additional conditions that separate identities, together with a positive realization (Theorem 10.2). That further result is not supplied merely by calling the complete set unique.
