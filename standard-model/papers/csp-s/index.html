Technical overview

A domain-sensitive mixed-use algebra constructs the greatest observational congruence for the declared target. Complete source reconstruction factors admitted covariant uses. On represented branches, tensor probes recover charged coefficients, and complex traces of words up to length seventeen classify two three-dimensional Hermitian quark Gram matrices up to simultaneous unitary conjugacy. The result separates structural-law closure from numerical parameter selection.
