BCRS-III / Bosonic responses

The Bosonic Carrier–Response–Projection Atlas

Amos Jay Maley / Published 2026-09-08

Joins electromagnetic, weak, color and Higgs responses on one source, retaining mixed information that is lost when the sectors are considered separately.

Technical overview

The complete mixed observation algebra gives an exact quotient and retains relative attachment data. Five invariant response coordinates reconstruct the stipulated joint local interaction, or six with the observed vacuum contribution. A fixed Wheeler-DeWitt matter branch supplies a simultaneous classical gauge-Higgs-gravity history; stronger state and asymptotic projections retain their independent source data.
