Complete publisher description
Distributed Carriage, Response Differentiation, and Source-Relative Physical Realization This paper develops a bearer-first presentation of the constraint-derived Standard Model. It addresses a question that is distinct from classifying matter and bosonic responses: what bears the physical content whose differentiated responses appear as matter, gauge, charge, generation, and Higgs roles? The paper constructs that bearer from the distributed persistence organization of the realized physical interior. Local configurations, boundary comparisons, transport, restoration, perturbation, and loop witnesses are established before species names or numerical spacetime geometry. The resulting persistence bearer is operational rather than passive: admissible changes can alter the standing-bearing transport, comparison, restoration, and identity organization it supports. The distributed bearer retains: bounded local support; standing and identity memory; restoration and perturbation structure; comparison and boundary traces; admitted transport; continuation and failure data; actual local configurations and overlap witnesses. Only participating local domains are included. No response is silently assigned to every formal domain, and no featureless global reservoir is introduced beneath the demonstrated persistence organization. The paper then reconstructs the Standard-Model architecture from this bearer. Gauge sectors are treated as source-defined extractions, representation and charge as witnessed responses, and the Higgs as a joint order-to-deformation mediation role. These roles are not introduced as independently carried substances; they arise through differentiated response constructions acting on one common distributed persistence organization. A complete native response trace is formed from the original admitted operations and tests. It records successful responses, activity status, unlicensed use, failure values, partial domains, boundary data, transport, loop closure, quotient behavior, and admitted continuations. The resulting response quotient identifies exactly those source presentations that the retained target cannot distinguish. The reconstruction preserves: partial operation domains; shared inputs and fixed-sibling data; relative source attachments; endpoint and loop information; admissibility and failure conditions; order, deformation, and mediation incidences; algebraic realizations and their witness-preserving comparisons; physical state, operator, and continuation authority. This establishes a faithful passage between the operational persistence source, the differentiated structural-law architecture, and the retained physical target. The reconstruction is deliberately source-relative: observational equality is not treated as source isomorphism, raw quotient maps are not inverted without authorization, and a vector-space or field presentation is not mistaken for a complete physical realization. The paper also connects the bearer-first reconstruction to the occupied quantum–Einstein source. The same source, state, operation, and authority data are preserved when the structural role system is compared with its common physical realization. Gravity retains its two distinct positions: an upstream constraint and localization role required for a coherent physical arena, and a later metric and dynamical realization on the compatible physical source. Gravity is therefore not counted as another matter or gauge carrier. A central result establishes that no additional same-target primitive material bearer is required by the established architecture. Any proposed domain-internal substrate that preserves the stipulated persistence organization, including support, standing, identity, restoration, comparison, boundary, and transport, realizes the already established distributed carriage. Any distinction with a standing-bearing consequence is classified through the retained source and role interface; a proposal that changes the target, source authority, boundary, or comparison regime is a different target rather than a same-target counterexample. This yields a precise form of universality: one common distributed persistence organization; multiple differentiated matter, gauge, charge, generation, and Higgs responses; faithful preservation of response distinctions; no need for an additional same-target primitive material bearer. The result does not collapse the response quotient to a singleton, identify all local realizations, or treat distinct roles as algebraically or physically indistinguishable. Universality of carriage coexists with genuine response differentiation. Scope and realization boundary The conclusions apply to the established structural-law and occupied physical targets, with their original source, comparison, boundary, continuation, state, and operator conditions retained. The paper does not assert that a reduced snapshot of current bearer profiles determines every future response, nor does it claim that all possible quantum theories, spectral branches, metrics, or gravitational extensions belong to the same source. Numerical masses, couplings, lifetimes, pole data, and state-dependent spectral quantities retain their own physical realization and determination requirements. The common-bearer result is therefore target-relative. It excludes an additional primitive bearer within the established architecture, while leaving legitimate extensions that change the target or introduce new source authority available for separate analysis. Role in the AASC programme CSP-R supplies the bearer-to-role reconstruction layer linking the realized physical interior to the FCGS, BCRS, and CSP structures. It explains how one distributed persistence organization can support differentiated matter, gauge, charge, generation, and Higgs roles without multiplying the primitive bearer. Its principal contribution is not a second derivation of each predecessor classification theorem, but a faithful reconstruction showing how the bearer, response quotient, source class, and physical realization fit together.
