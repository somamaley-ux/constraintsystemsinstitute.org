Electroweak and Higgs Realization from a Premetric Response Atlas

Amos Jay Maley / Manuscript

Follows the common Higgs-gauge source into electroweak fixation, residual charge, response supports, local interactions, and source-qualified physical channels.

Technical overview

On the one-doublet branch, residual action and the kinetic form determine gauge supports; with the scalar normal quotient they form a four-atom graded support lattice. Four invariant coordinates reconstruct the normalized local interaction, with a fifth when vacuum value is observed. Joint source kernels and complete continuation data retain physical state, channel and operator-domain information.
