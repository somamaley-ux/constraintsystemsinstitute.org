Classical Standard Model and Gravity from a Common Constraint-Governed Source

Amos Jay Maley / 2026-09-05 / Manuscript

Constructs a common classical Standard Model-gravity branch with an attached total source, exact coupled constraints, local evolution, and finite-algebra classical fermions.

Technical overview

A fixed covariant action occupies both a represented polynomial chart and an Einstein-matter construction on a compact hyperbolic Cauchy surface with a scalar clock. The exact Dirac-bracket constraint algebra, mixed-order right inverse and finite refoliation theorem concern the stated local regular body. Finite exterior-algebra lifting constructs classical anticommuting-field solutions. This branch is separately specified from CSP-P's occupied physical source.
