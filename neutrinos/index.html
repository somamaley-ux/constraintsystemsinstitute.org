02 / Why vectorlike?

Build the heavy connection.

Start with two matching field components. Add an allowed mass pairing, then connect them to the light weak sector.

The completed connection

A direct mass and a connection to light matter.

The heavy components have matching gauge charges, allowing a direct Dirac mass pairing. A separate Higgs/Yukawa coupling connects the right-chiral heavy component to the left-chiral light lepton doublet.

Next: match the signals →

This matching of the left and right gauge identities is what vectorlike means here.

The source terms, chirality, and the two symmetry branches

PCR5E §8.2 constructs equal-rank singlet multiplets NL and NR, together with three νR fields. The heavy left- and right-chiral singlets transform in the same Standard Model representation (1, 1, 0) and both have conventional B−L = −1. The two displayed operators are:

−N̄L MN NR + h.c.
−L̄ H̃ YLN NR + h.c.

The first is a gauge-invariant direct Dirac mass term. The second connects the Standard Model lepton doublet to the heavy singlet through the Higgs field. The complete source also contains the νR Yukawa term and NL–νR mixing. Its common mass-map factorization, active incidences and retained physical relations supply the later light/heavy structure.

“Vectorlike” specifies how chiral components transform. The Dirac/Majorana distinction concerns mass and particle–antiparticle structure; it is a separate question. In the exact-global B−L child, the symmetry forbids the retained lepton-number-violating response. The bounded-accidental child preserves B−L through operator dimension four and admits additional higher-dimensional lepton-number-violating responses. A null search alone does not select between them.

This exhibit isolates selected field types and source operators. It neither counts heavy states nor derives carrier identity from a pairing diagram. What is robust, and under which conditions?

Exact heavy degeneracy has zero unrestricted symmetry-breaking radius: a nonzero traceless Hermitian perturbation can separate the support. Exact flavor universality likewise requires equal singular values of the charged-current heavy block. Neither property follows from the other.

Rank three persists for a sufficiently small perturbation of a full-rank heavy coupling, with a separate condition retaining the heavy spectral cluster. The closure identity Δ′act = Ω′H holds on the complete block-isometry class. One terminal carrier persists on a certified source-support and continuation domain. A claim that all three survive together uses the intersection of those domains.

Here Ω′H = δ diag(1 + a, 1, 1 − a), with 0 ≤ a ≤ 0.6, and the light weights are chosen as its normalized complement. Rank and closure follow by construction. The sliders do not certify a physical source-support domain. Internal splitting alone supplies no independently complete second carrier.

The experimental benchmark and the remaining inputs

The earlier D3 study supplies a 28–30 GeV search benchmark with a 0.2 mm response-chart lifetime coordinate. Only 30 GeV is a direct ATLAS mass anchor in that band. These are not a derived numerical mass or proper lifetime for the final vectorlike carrier. The final structural heavy domain remains symbolic, and a bridge identifying it with that benchmark has not been supplied.

A physical unstable-state pole and lifetime require the full propagator/self-energy and response construction. A detector peak or unresolved line is not enough to determine exact degeneracy. A real test also needs all relevant production and decay categories, weak normalization, nuisance and correlation handling, and certified coverage.
