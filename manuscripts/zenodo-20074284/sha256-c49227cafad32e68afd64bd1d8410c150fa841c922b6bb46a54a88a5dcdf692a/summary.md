# The Structural Inadmissibility of the Null Regime

> Attributed reading aid, not a complete manuscript conversion or independent proof verification.

Establishes a fixed-domain closure theorem excluding total vacuity as an admissibility-bearing evaluability regime under the manuscript’s same-type preservation conditions. The result is closure-theoretic rather than an existential or cosmological construction.
