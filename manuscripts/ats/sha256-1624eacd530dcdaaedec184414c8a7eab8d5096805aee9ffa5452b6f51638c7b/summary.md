## Imports

States a one-way order: Kernel, AMetric, Structure, fixed-domain exhaustion, then ATS. References name kernel v49, AMetric editorial reference, Structure examples reference 20 September and Exhaustion composition reference 20 September.

## Establishes

Decomposes a fixed-domain admissible description into context (Anchor), complete semantic content (Tensor) and identity-preserving realization (Skin), with reconstruction maps; characterizes minimal realization uniqueness up to commuting bijection and finite-use envelopes/substitution.

## Supplies downstream

Supplies a typed organization for assembling/checking admissible descriptions and certificates. It does not serve as a premise for the earlier general exhaustion theorem.

## Scope and limits

A complete semantic profile is not merely an admissibility bit and need not identify a unique occupant. Minimal-realization uniqueness does not imply uniqueness among every adequate realization. Sequential substitution requires joint original-domain factorization; a licence alone is not execution.
