Formalism and dependencies

From determinate construction to licensed description

The manuscripts argue that non-degenerate determinate construction incurs a necessary kernel of Admissibility, Standing, Reference and Irreversibility. This guide attributes those claims and identifies verified reading editions; it is not an independent proof audit.

Reading order is editorial. Dependency types distinguish logical support, local reconstruction, projection and certificate assembly. Older reference editions are named as cited by consumers; their exact imported bytes are unconfirmed and their PDFs are not hosted here.

Non-Degenerate Construction and the Kernel of Admissibility

Reference edition · 2026-09-26

Imports
Begins with non-degenerate determinate construction and original incidence. It does not assume the downstream binary interface, occupancy or ATS decomposition as premises.
Establishes
The manuscript derives necessary Admissibility, Standing, Reference and Irreversibility roles from incurred objecthood obligations, and proves functional minimality and same-object exhaustion. Exact realization requires preservation of original observations through the encoding fibres.
Supplies downstream
Supplies a kernel necessity argument and a representation-faithfulness criterion. Downstream September papers cite version 49, rather than the preferred 26 September PDF.
Limits
The functional roles are not an optional overlay. Agreement under complete queries requires independent individual warrants. Unique or absent outcomes are not a blanket assertion about all research or every question; semantic absence is not computational timeout.

The Structure of Admissibility

Publication reference · 2026-09-21

Imports
Reconstructs kernel version 49 and AMetric boundary arguments locally; §1.4 distinguishes this support from using later conclusions as premises.
Establishes
Bivalent qualification is an exact quotient only when all required original observations are constant on the bit fibres. It constructs the greatest safe interior relative to independently fixed qualification and continuation edges, and gives finite failed-path and exhaustion witnesses.
Supplies downstream
Supplies complete-profile and continuation-interior machinery used in fixed-domain exhaustion and referenced by ATS. The downstream cited edition is the 20 September examples reference.
Limits
The interior may be empty. Uniqueness concerns the complete/maximal interior for fixed data, not every safe subset or a unique physical occupant. Failure retention and negative endpoint absorption have different conditions.

Reference edition · 2026-09-20

Imports
Locally uses kernel necessity (version 49 in ref.13). The Structure comparison in ref.18 is not a logical back-edge from Structure into the boundary theorem.
Establishes
At a fixed authoritative equality-only atom interface, invariant readouts cannot recover additional distinctions without warranted richer input. Theorem 7.5 states the construction-neutral exclusion package with explicit accommodation of independently warranted enrichment.

Supplies the conditional boundary/exclusion result and export discipline. Metricity applies a boundary package to physical metric licensing; the exact AMetric edition imported by the May Metricity paper is unresolved.

Metricity as Licensed Description

May 2026 manuscript · 2026-05

Imports
Names Kernel, Structure, AMetric and ATS companions without edition-identifying hashes/dates. Its AMetric exclusion package is a premise of conditional licensing; new September editions must not be silently assigned as the May paper’s imports.
Establishes
Distinguishes weak formal/model/calculational interpretation from strong physical interpretation, defines the local L-metric projection, and gives conditional licensing for physical metric description.
Supplies downstream
Supplies the distinction between a metric expression and warranted physical relata, records, calibration and interpretation; it offers a licensing branch, rather than an additional premise in the fixed-domain decomposition spine.
Limits
Definitions 2.7-2.11 permit weak formal interpretation before physical standing. Corollary 6.3 says licensing does not generate metric structure, standing or ontology. The projection order is a dependency order, not a temporal sequence, and no globally unique metric interface is claimed.

Anchor, Tensor, and Skin

Publication reference · 2026-09-21

Imports
States a one-way order: Kernel, AMetric, Structure, fixed-domain exhaustion, then ATS. References name kernel v49, AMetric editorial reference, Structure examples reference 20 September and Exhaustion composition reference 20 September.
Establishes
Decomposes a fixed-domain admissible description into context (Anchor), complete semantic content (Tensor) and identity-preserving realization (Skin), with reconstruction maps; characterizes minimal realization uniqueness up to commuting bijection and finite-use envelopes/substitution.
Supplies downstream
Supplies a typed organization for assembling/checking admissible descriptions and certificates. It does not serve as a premise for the earlier general exhaustion theorem.
Limits
A complete semantic profile is not merely an admissibility bit and need not identify a unique occupant. Minimal-realization uniqueness does not imply uniqueness among every adequate realization. Sequential substitution requires joint original-domain factorization; a licence alone is not execution.

A Fixed-Domain Exhaustion Theorem

Publication reference · 2026-09-21

Imports
Reuses the 20 September Structure profile/interior results with short local proofs. Ref.6 names kernel v49; ref.7 names AMetric editorial reference. ATS is a comparison and is explicitly not assumed.
Establishes
Adds compositional coverage and joint-constructor descent to complete semantic profiles; exhausts specified failure-witness families under typed boundary discipline. Separates original obligation coverage, closure and candidate identification.
Supplies downstream
Supplies the finite-use compositional foundation and candidate readout criterion imported by ATS. ATS cites the 20 September composition reference rather than this preferred 21 September publication reference.
Limits
Arbitrary semantic coordinates are not automatically a finite implementation. Candidate-set constant readout, single occupant, and unique decomposition are separate results with separate assumptions. Retaining a failure history does not itself force every later endpoint negative; the edge 0-to-0 condition is additional.

Working glossary

These reading aids are scoped by the detailed manuscript citations above and do not replace their definitions.

Admissibility
Bivalent qualification relative to the fixed original question; an exact bit quotient requires all required observations to be constant on its fibres.
Standing
Warrant to bear the relevant role or target interpretation; weak calculational meaning does not itself supply physical standing.
Reference
Identity and original-incidence preservation across representations and reuse.
Irreversibility
Retention of original qualification and constraint history. Negative endpoint absorption is a separate additional edge condition.
Complete profile
All required semantic coordinates on the declared original domain, rather than only a yes/no bit.
Admissible interior
Greatest safe continuation region for fixed independent qualification and edge data; it may be empty.
Anchor / Tensor / Skin
Context / complete semantic content / identity-preserving realization in the fixed-domain decomposition.
Work DOI / exact release DOI
All-versions discovery identity / a particular verified publisher release.
