An Exhaustion Theorem for Standing, Reference, and Irreversibility

The manuscript derives necessary Admissibility, Standing, Reference and Irreversibility roles from incurred objecthood obligations, and proves functional minimality and same-object exhaustion. Exact realization requires preservation of original observations through the encoding fibres.

About this paper and citation
Imports
Begins with non-degenerate determinate construction and original incidence. It does not assume the downstream binary interface, occupancy or ATS decomposition as premises.
Supplies downstream
Supplies a kernel necessity argument and a representation-faithfulness criterion. Downstream September papers cite version 49, rather than the preferred 26 September PDF.
Scope and limits
The functional roles are not an optional overlay. Agreement under complete queries requires independent individual warrants. Unique or absent outcomes are not a blanket assertion about all research or every question; semantic absence is not computational timeout.
