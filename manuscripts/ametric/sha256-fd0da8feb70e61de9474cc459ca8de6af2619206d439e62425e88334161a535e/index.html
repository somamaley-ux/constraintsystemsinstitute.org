Supplies the conditional boundary/exclusion result and export discipline. Metricity applies a boundary package to physical metric licensing; the exact AMetric edition imported by the May Metricity paper is unresolved.
Scope and limits
Formal symbols, carriers, equality patterns and tuple relations are available before target-specific physical standing. Proposition 3.8 allows invariant formal structures (including equality-based discrete metrics). Theorem 7.5 permits independently warranted richer inputs; it does not rule out every formal distinction, all metric calculations or all research programs.
