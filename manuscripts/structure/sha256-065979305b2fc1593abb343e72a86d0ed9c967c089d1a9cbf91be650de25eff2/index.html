The Structure of Admissibility

Bivalent Qualification, Exhaustion, and the Unique Admissible Interior

Amos Jay Maley · 46 pages

Read paperDownload PDFDOI

Bivalent qualification is an exact quotient only when all required original observations are constant on the bit fibres. It constructs the greatest safe interior relative to independently fixed qualification and continuation edges, and gives finite failed-path and exhaustion witnesses.

About this paper and citation
Imports
Reconstructs kernel version 49 and AMetric boundary arguments locally; §1.4 distinguishes this support from using later conclusions as premises.
Supplies downstream
Supplies complete-profile and continuation-interior machinery used in fixed-domain exhaustion and referenced by ATS. The downstream cited edition is the 20 September examples reference.
Scope and limits
The interior may be empty. Uniqueness concerns the complete/maximal interior for fixed data, not every safe subset or a unique physical occupant. Failure retention and negative endpoint absorption have different conditions.
